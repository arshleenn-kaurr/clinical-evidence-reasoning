## MEDAL-derived material

The project's frozen cohorts and prompts derive from the **MEDAL** dataset, specifically:
- `data/QA-systematic-review.jsonl`: a MEDAL export of Cochrane systematic-review QA pairs.
- `data/recommendation-aha-guideline-clean.jsonl`: a MEDAL export of AHA/Red Cross first-aid guideline recommendations.

**Redistribution status under review.** MEDAL's own license/terms of use were not located in the recovered research material and have not been independently reviewed as part of this repository's work. See `docs/licensing_decision_matrix.md`.

## Cochrane-derived material

Content ultimately sourced from Cochrane systematic reviews (via the MEDAL export above): the anchor text and reported effect estimates in the main/replication/latent cohorts, and the Cochrane source notes in the candidate-audit CSVs. Cochrane's own copyright and redistribution terms were not independently reviewed.

**Redistribution status under review.**

## AHA-derived material

Content ultimately sourced from American Heart Association / American Red Cross first-aid guideline recommendations (via the MEDAL export above): the veto/boundary cohort's anchor text and the AHA candidate-audit CSV. AHA's own copyright terms were not independently reviewed.

**Redistribution status under review.**

## Provider model outputs

Raw and canonical model outputs (`results/*.jsonl` across all three accepted-paper packages) were generated by:
- **Google Gemini API** (`gemini-3.6-flash`, and historically `gemini-2.5-flash`), via `generateContent`.
- **Groq** (`openai/gpt-oss-120b`), via Groq's chat completions endpoint.

Each provider's own terms of service govern redistribution of model outputs generated through their API; these terms were not independently reviewed as part of this repository's work.

**Redistribution status under review.**
